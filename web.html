I Want to be an Astronaut!

Hello there! Have you ever looked up at the night sky and wondered what it is like to visit the stars? My absolute biggest dream is to become an astronaut when I grow up!

What Does an Astronaut Do?

Astronauts are super-cool space explorers. They ride inside giant, loud rockets that zoom super fast into outer space! Once they are way up high, they get to live and work inside a spaceship.

The most fun part is that things float in space! Because there is no gravity holding them down, astronauts can float around like balloons. They can do flips in the air and even try to catch floating drops of water in their mouths.

Why is it the Best Job Ever?

I think being an astronaut is the best job because you get to wear an awesome, shiny spacesuit. You also get to look out the window and see our home, planet Earth, looking like a giant blue and green marble.

Top 5 Reasons Being an Astronaut is Awesome:

  1. You get to float in space!
  2. You get to wear a cool spacesuit.
  3. You get to see Earth from space.
  4. You get to ride in a rocket ship.
  5. You get to explore the unknown!
